Our Story

Born from a love of matcha.

Hunnie Matcha started as a simple obsession — finding the perfect matcha drink. After countless cups and experimenting with recipes, we decided to bring our creations to the community.

Every week you'll find us at local farmers markets and events, serving ceremonial grade matcha drinks made to order.

We believe in quality ingredients, slow preparation, and a moment of calm in your busy day. Come say hi — we'd love to make your next cup.
